## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, resilient, and forward-looking digital presence for the Syrian Journalists Union (اتحاد الصحفيين السوريين) on Android. Rooted in the principles of investigative clarity, press freedom, and civic dignity, the aesthetic balances the gravitas of institutional journalism with modern mobile ergonomics. 

The stylistic direction blends **Refined Institutional Modernism** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- **Atmospheric Nocturne:** A nocturnal baseline composed of deep obsidian and ink-slate tones creates an eye-resting environment for media workers consuming field dispatches, urgent press alerts, and long-form investigative files in low-light environments.
- **Luminous Anchors:** Expressive accents inspired by Syrian heritage and journalistic valor—deep ceremonial emerald balanced with radioactive phosphor greens, antique golden amber for honors and accreditation badges, and crisp glacial cyan for verified syndication metrics and encryption notices.
- **Material Precision:** Grounded in Android Material You design patterns with native Right-to-Left (RTL) fluid dynamics, surface-level elevation via translucent glass scrims, hairline neon borders, and controlled ambient glows that guide thumb navigation.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep OLED performance, field readability, and distinct institutional hierarchy:

### Surface & Neutral Architecture
- **Surface Void (`#080e1a`):** Base canvas and system status bar background.
- **Surface Substrate (`#0f172a`):** Standard sheet and primary card background.
- **Surface Elevated (`#162036`):** Floating sheets, app bars, dialogs, and active card layers.
- **Surface Highlight (`#22324e`):** Selected items, inactive segmented controls, and input backplates.

### Accent Roles
- **Primary Emerald (`#10b981` / `#059669` / `#0b3d2a`):** Symbolizes growth, independence, and verified syndicate membership. Used for primary CTAs, active tab icons, cryptographic verification states, and verified press seals.
- **Syndicate Amber (`#c9a24a` / `#f59e0b`):** Reflects institutional prestige, archival honor rolls, official press card tiers, legal defense alerts, and warnings.
- **Cyan / Glacial Glow (`#38bdf8` / `#0284c7`):** Direct communication signals, live transmission indicators, real-time wire notifications, and telemetry metadata.
- **Critical Alert (`#ef4444`):** Emergency distress beacon, SOS signaling for field journalists, and censorship tracking flags.

### Text Contrast & Readability
- **Text High Emphasis:** `#f8fafc` (95% opacity) for titles, articles, and primary labels.
- **Text Medium Emphasis:** `#94a3b8` (70% opacity) for timestamps, author bylines, and subtitles.
- **Text Disabled/Muted:** `#475569` (45% opacity) for inactive states and non-interactive borders.

## Typography

The typography is built around **Cairo**, an Arabic geometric typeface with calligraphic proportions perfectly balanced for digital interfaces, dense news copy, and Arabic RTL script characteristics.

### Guidelines for Arabic Script Setting
- **Line Heights:** Arabic diacritics and taller ascenders/descenders require 20–30% larger line-height ratios than Latin equivalents to prevent overlapping markings (Tashkeel).
- **Font Weight Hierarchy:** 
  - `800 (ExtraBold)` for breaking dispatches and dynamic lead counters.
  - `700 (Bold)` for major section titles and syndicate statement headers.
  - `600 (SemiBold)` for contextual labels, metadata chips, and navigation items.
  - `400 (Regular)` strictly for long-form journalistic bodies, official legal texts, and press statements.
- **RTL Nuances:** Tracking and letter-spacing are set to `0` or slightly positive (`+0.01em`) exclusively on headings; letter-spacing must never be detached on connected Arabic cursives.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for native Android RTL dynamics, anchoring content starting from the right margin across a fluid single-to-dual column mobile frame:

### Spatial Logic
- **Outer Canvas Margins:** `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports ensures comfortable thumb resting zones on curved edge displays and accommodates Android edge-to-edge system gestures.
- **Internal Gutters:** `1rem` (16px) separation between parallel list items, feed cards, and grid metrics.
- **RTL Fluidity:** Structural directionality defaults to `dir="rtl"`. Action icons (back arrows, disclosure indicators) mirror horizontally, while asymmetric time-series markers and status badges lead from the right edge.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 4px baseline grid unit applied strictly across all vertical dimensions (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 36px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid of **Tonal Layering**, **Atmospheric Glowing Borders**, and **Subtle Luminous Diffusion** rather than muddy dropshadows:

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#080e1a` solid void; sits behind the status bar and native Android navigation bar.
- **Level 1 (Substrate Cards):** `#0f172a` bordered with 1px hairline stroke in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Surfaces):** `#162036` overlay with 1px hairline stroke in `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)` or `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets & Overlays):** `#162036` backed with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an ambient top rim glow.

### Luminescence & Glow Signatures
- **Press Verification State:** `box-shadow: 0px 0px 16px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`
- **Syndicate Urgent Bulletin:** `box-shadow: 0px 0px 20px -2px rgba(201, 162, 74, 0.35)`
- **Live Dispatch Broadcast:** `box-shadow: 0px 0px 14px 0px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- Standard UI controls, inputs, and interactive list items inherit `0.5rem` (8px).
- Content cards, digital member press identity passes, and bottom sheets adopt `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Dynamic action pills, floating badges, and circular operational toggles use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) or full stadium curves.

This curvature balances modern tactile smartphone ergonomics with the structure needed for official union credentials and multi-column article previews.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA:** Background in bright emerald `#10b981` with text in `#080e1a` (Bold Cairo), 48px standard touch target height, 8px corner radius. Active state provides a slight compression scale (0.98) and an emerald halo ring (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`).
- **Institutional / Amber CTA:** Solid `#c9a24a` background with `#080e1a` text; used exclusively for press accreditation renewal, legal emergency dispatch, and certification requests.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Transparent backplate, 1px border in `#22324e`, text in `#f8fafc`, elevated hover/touch fill to `#162036`.

### Cards & Press Identification Pass
- **Digital Press Card (بطاقة العضوية):** Premium card-based layout featuring a gradient shift from `#0b3d2a` to `#162036`, overlaid with a 1px metallic hairline border in `#c9a24a`. Features QR verification badge on the left, journalist photo and Arabic credentials on the right, accompanied by an active green authenticity pulse.
- **Newsfeed & Dossier Card:** `#0f172a` body, hairline separation, right-aligned thumbnail or leading media strip, metadata row (category, reading duration, timestamp) displayed with muted cyan tags.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container:** Dark slate foundation (`#162036`), 1px outline in `#22324e`.
- **Focus State:** 1px stroke shift to emerald `#10b981` with a diffuse `0 0 10px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` edge glow.
- **Label Placement:** Native RTL alignment; placeholder text rendered in `#94a3b8` with clear inline icons shifted to the left edge (leading in RTL context).

### Badges, Chips & Status Indicators
- **Verification Seal (توثيق):** Pill-shaped chip, surface `#0b3d2a`, text `#10b981`, leading checkmark shield icon.
- **Status Indicator Dots:** 8px glowing discs displaying live transmission (`#38bdf8`), active union standing (`#10b981`), or pending dossier review (`#c9a24a`).
- **Emergency Distress Banner:** Top-docked alert strip in `#ef4444` deep tint with a high-contrast white text banner and rapid-action icon for field safety reporting.

### Navigation Elements
- **App Bar:** Edge-to-edge transparent-to-solid transition (`#080e1a` at 90% opacity with backdrop blur), centered institutional emblem, RTL back-chevron on the right, and notification bell on the left.
- **Android Bottom Navigation Bar:** Deep slate `#0f172a` container with an emerald-highlighted indicator pill for the active destination, maintaining 56px minimum ergonomic clearance above the Android system home bar.
